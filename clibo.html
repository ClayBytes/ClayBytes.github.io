Clibo AI Companion

Floating Overlay without Context-Switching for Android

Clibo AI Companion is an intelligent Android assistant developed by ClayBytes. Operating as an interactive system overlay over any active Android application, Clibo brings instant AI assistance directly to where you work, browse, or chat without toggling apps.

Why Clibo AI Companion?

Traditional mobile AI apps force users to switch applications, copy and paste text, and break their flow. Clibo solves this by bringing AI intelligence directly onto your screen as a lightweight floating widget.

🪟

1. Floating System Overlay

Powered by the flutter_overlay_window engine, Clibo sits unobtrusively on your screen. Tap once to open an interactive AI assistant over any app.

🔀

2. Multi-Provider AI Routing

Choose the ideal AI model for your work: Google Gemini (3.5 Flash Lite / 3.8 Flash), OpenAI (gpt-4o-mini), Anthropic Claude (claude-3-5-sonnet), or a local Ollama server.

📷

3. Multimodal Screen Analysis

Let Clibo analyze what's on your screen. Take single-tap screenshots to summarize documents, explain charts, debug code, or answer questions instantly.

🛡️

4. Privacy & Encrypted Local Storage

Built with privacy at its core. User data and chat history are saved with local device encryption. Screenshots are processed ephemerally upon request.

Supported AI Models & Backend Architecture

Clibo communicates via a secure Gateway API for request verification, usage telemetry, end-to-end TLS encryption, and dynamic LLM routing.
