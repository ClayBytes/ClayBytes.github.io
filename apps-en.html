Clibo AI Companion

Context-Aware, Zero-Context-Switching Floating AI Companion for Android

Clibo is an AI companion designed for Android that floats seamlessly as a system overlay over any running application (such as Snapchat, Instagram, Chrome, or Notes). Query AI, analyze on-screen content, or draft replies instantly without leaving your current app.

Android Overlay Multi-LLM Routing Multimodal Vision Local Ollama Support Privacy-First

Core Features of Clibo

Architected for speed, model flexibility, and user privacy.

📱

Floating System Overlay

Instant AI access over any running Android app using flutter_overlay_window. No context switching or app toggling required while chatting, browsing, or reading.

⚡

Multi-Provider AI Routing

Seamlessly switch between Google Gemini (3.5 Flash Lite & 3.8 Flash), a local Ollama container on your own network, OpenAI (gpt-4o-mini), and Anthropic Claude (claude-3-5-sonnet).

👁️

Multi-Modal Screen Understanding

Capture screenshots with a single tap. Let Clibo analyze documents, charts, code, or visual content displayed on your screen in real time.

🔒

Privacy & Guardrails

On-device encrypted storage, explicit screen capture consent logging, metered usage telemetry, and built-in rate-limiting guardrails.
