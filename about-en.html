About Clayton Reitz

Agentic AI Architect & Fractional CTO with 17+ years of experience delivering scalable, secure, and high-performance systems across enterprise engineering, real-time cloud infrastructure, high-frequency trading, and agentic AI.

Who I Am & My Background

I am Clayton Reitz — Agentic AI Architect, Fractional CTO, and Senior Software Architect based in Zaandam, Netherlands.

In 2012, I completed my Bachelor of Arts in Social Science & Psychology at the University of the Western Cape (UWC). Driven to connect human-centered ethics with technology, I immediately pursued a Postgraduate Diploma in Information Technology (PGDIT) in Software Engineering.

Starting my professional software engineering career in 2013 at Santam LTD, I contributed to core mainframe-to-Guidewire insurance modernizations, followed by high-scale healthcare messaging integrations at Metropolitan Health Group (IBM MQ, IIB v8, WebSphere). Relocating to the Netherlands, I delivered mission-critical systems for Prodin B.V., DICTU, Belastingdienst (Dutch Tax Authority), Detransparantebroker.nl, ABB E-Mobility, Omnia Retail, and Gardsign.nl.

Today, through ClayBytes, I advise companies as a Fractional CTO and Agentic AI Architect. My recent innovations include publishing Clibo AI Companion (floating Android AI overlay), building deep-agent AI orchestration platforms (Spring AI, LangGraph, MCP, LangSmith), sub-millisecond Solana MEV execution engines in Rust (HFT), and AI-driven legacy code translation architectures ("Rosetta Stone").
