About ClayBytes

ClayBytes is an independent IT consultancy founded by Clayton Reitz, helping individuals, small businesses, influencers, startups, and organizations build scalable, secure, and future‑ready digital systems. With more than 15 years of experience in enterprise engineering, modern frontends, and AI‑driven automation, I deliver solutions designed to last.

Who I Am & My Journey

I am Clayton Reitz — an independent software architect, senior engineer, and agentic AI practitioner based in Zaandam, Netherlands. Born in the rural regions of the Karoo, Koue Bokkeveld, and Ceres in South Africa, I grew up far from technology but deeply drawn to understanding both human behavior and complex systems.

In 2012, I graduated with a Bachelor of Arts from the University of the Western Cape (UWC), majoring in Psychology, Social Science, Ethics, and Anthropology. Driven to bridge human behavior with technology, I immediately pivoted into a postgraduate diploma in Information Technology (PGDIT) in Software Engineering. Lacking hostel placement at UWC, I literally lived under the stairs in the main campus cafeteria during my studies — powered by sheer grit and a refusal to be deterred.

In 2013, I began my professional software engineering career at Santam LTD (Guidewire & C# .NET integration), followed by enterprise integration roles at Metropolitan Health Group (IBM MQ, IIB v8, WebSphere). That foundation eventually brought me to the Netherlands, where I contributed to mission-critical systems for the **Dutch Tax Authority (Belastingdienst)**, **DICTU**, **ABB E-Mobility**, **Omnia Retail**, and **Gardsign.nl**.

Today, through ClayBytes, I combine deep human-centered ethics with enterprise software engineering. My latest achievements include developing **Clibo AI Companion** (floating Android overlay AI), architecting deep-agent AI workflows (LangGraph, MCP, LangSmith), and engineering high-frequency trading (HFT) sub-millisecond Solana execution engines.
