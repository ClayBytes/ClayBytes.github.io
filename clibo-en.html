Clibo AI Companion

Zero-Context-Switching Floating Overlay for Android

Clibo AI Companion is an intelligent Android assistant created by ClayBytes. Floating as an interactive system overlay on top of any running Android application, Clibo brings instant AI assistance directly to where you work, browse, or message without requiring context switching.

Android Overlay System Google Gemini OpenAI Anthropic Claude Ollama Local AI Multimodal Vision
📲 Download Beta via Firebase App Distribution

Why Clibo AI Companion?

Traditional AI mobile apps force users to switch apps, copy and paste text, and disrupt their workflow. Clibo solves this by bringing AI intelligence directly onto your screen as a lightweight floating widget.

🪟

1. Floating Overlay System

Powered by the flutter_overlay_window engine, Clibo sits unobtrusively on screen. Tap once to bring up an interactive AI assistant over any app.

🔀

2. Multi-Provider AI Routing

Select the ideal AI model for your workflow: Google Gemini (3.5 Flash Lite / 3.8 Flash), OpenAI (gpt-4o-mini), Anthropic Claude (claude-3-5-sonnet), or a local Ollama server.

📷

3. Multimodal Screen Understanding

Allow Clibo to inspect on-screen context. Capture screenshots with one tap to summarize documents, explain charts, debug code, or answer questions instantly.

🛡️

4. Privacy & Encrypted Local Storage

Built with privacy at its core. User data and chat history are stored with on-device encryption. Screen captures are processed ephemerally on request.

Supported AI Models & Backend Architecture

Clibo interfaces with a secure Production Gateway API (https://clibobe-277710406862.europe-west4.run.app/api/v1) for request verification, usage metering guardrails, and dynamic model routing.
